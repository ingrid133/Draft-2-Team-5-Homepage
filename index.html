Frequently Asked Questions

Is academic coaching free?

Yes. Academic coaching is a free resource for eligible CU Boulder students.

How do I schedule an appointment?

Visit Buff Portal Advising, sign in, select your academic coach, and choose an available appointment.

What if I don't have a coach?

Contact Academic Coaching to learn about available coaching options.
